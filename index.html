Questions & Answers

What’s the dress code?

Cocktail attire is suggested for the welcome dinner. For the wedding day, formal attire is requested (black tie optional).

How do I get to the venue? Is there parking?

Transportation to the wedding venue will be provided for all guests. If you prefer to arrive by taxi or car, onsite parking is available.

What will the weather be like?

Spring in Verona is generally mild, though evenings can be cooler. Our welcome dinner will be hosted outdoors, and both the wedding day ceremony and cocktail hour are also planned to take place outside. We recommend bringing a light jacket or wrap. The reception and after-party will be held indoors.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Please include any dietary restrictions or allergies on your RSVP, and we will coordinate with the venue and caterer to ensure you are well accommodated.

When is the best time to book flights?

Based on current fare trends, we recommend booking flights by January 26. Flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday often offers the best pricing. While Verona has a convenient regional airport, it is quite small. You may find better prices by flying into Venice or Milan and continuing to Verona by train.

A gentle reminder: passports sneak up on all of us—please make sure yours is current.
